## 🎨 Design & Styling
- **Background**: Solid black (`#000000`).
- **Cards**: Vibrant ice blue gradient (`#e0f2fe` to `#7dd3fc`) with crisp cyan border (`#38bdf8`) and luminous shadows.
- **Layout**: Clean vertical stack (`flex-direction: column`) arranged one by one going down.
- **Cursor**: Simple, native browser cursor.
- **2D Effects**: Smooth, subtle elevation on hover with no 3D tilt or perspective transforms.
